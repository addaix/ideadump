ideadump.base#

Errors and constants shared across ideadump.

An idea is one folder in the store, ideas/<id>/, holding its record (idea.json, the single source of truth), a rendered README.md that a person reads on GitHub or a phone, and its research notes under research/. Nothing here depends on any other module.

ideadump.base.DEPTHS = ('light', 'deep')#

How deep a research note went. light is the pass made at capture (minutes, a few sources); deep is a full do-research-style report.
