ideadump.search#

Rank ideas against a query: BM25 over words, standard library only.

The query can be a few words (“offline speech to text”) or a page of text describing recent work (ledgers, issue titles, commit messages): long queries are the “is anything I’ve been working on related to my ideas?” case. The scorer is a seam: any score(query, docs) -> list[float] can be passed to rank(), for instance a semantic scorer backed by the fleet’s ir search.

>>> docs = {"a": "offline speech to text on a phone", "b": "a recipe for bread"}
>>> [hit["id"] for hit in rank("speech recognition offline", docs)]
['a']
